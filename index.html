Backup & import

⇩

Export

Download all flora and fauna observations, including photographs, as one backup file. The CSV contains the section you are viewing (flora) as text only.

⇧

Import

Restore a JSON backup (both sections), or import a CSV into the section you are viewing. CSV columns: family, genus, species, dateObserved, locality, floweringSeason (flora only) and notes.

☍

Storage

Observations are kept in this browser on this device only. Works on laptops and Android phones: use Export on one device and Import on the other to move your records.
